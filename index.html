Will this month's money cover every payable?

AsaKwarta is a monthly cash-flow planner for families and anyone who shares the bills. It is not an expense tracker. You list what has to be paid this month, each with its due date, and the money coming in, and AsaKwarta shows whether there is enough before each due date.

How it works

  1. Set up once. Add your monthly payables with their due dates, such as rent, school fees, the helper's salary and the levy, plus your credit cards and your salaries with their pay days.
  2. Each month, fill in what changes. When a card statement comes out, type in the amount. Add one-off extras, like a birthday party, and any cash you have on hand, with the day it comes in.
  3. See where you stand. The plan starts from what is in the bank today, adds what is still coming in, takes off what is still to pay, and shows what is left at the end of the month. Every payable is listed by due date with what is left after it.

What it does for you

  • Warns you early if money will run short before a payday, and by how much at worst.
  • Reminds you when a card statement is out and before every due date.
  • Tick payables off as you pay them, including part payments like a card minimum.
  • Look ahead to next month, or any month, with every bill already in it.
  • Keep a separate plan for each country, each in its own currency, or see them all together.
  • Share one plan with your family or group. Nobody joins without the owner's approval.
  • Works offline, and changes sync when you are back online.

Private by design

AsaKwarta never connects to your bank or cards and never asks for card numbers, bank logins or PINs. Everything in it is what you type in. There are no ads and no tracking. Read the Privacy Policy.

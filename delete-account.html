Delete your AsaKwarta account

You can delete your account and data yourself in the app, or ask us to do it for you.

In the app

  1. Open AsaKwarta and sign in.
  2. On the Plan tab, tap the Settings gear at the top right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and tap Delete account.

Without the app

Email asakwarta.admin@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject Delete my AsaKwarta account. We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in: email address, name, and your link to Google sign-in if you used it.
  • Any request you made to join a group.
  • If you are the only member of your group: the group and everything in it, including income, bills, credit cards, statement amounts, extras, cash on hand and its log, payment history and exchange rates.

What is kept

  • If you share your group with others, the group's plan stays for them. Only you are removed.
  • Encrypted weekly database backups, kept only for disaster recovery, may still hold a copy until they expire after at most 90 days.
  • Backup files you saved yourself, for example in Google Drive, are yours to delete.

See also the Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

AsaKwarta is a monthly cash-flow planner. You type in your income, bills, credit card amounts, cash on hand and other payables, and the app shows what is in the bank today, what is still to pay, and whether your money covers each payable before its due date. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and the name you choose to show to your group. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google email address and name. We never see your Google or email password.
  • Your plan: what you type into the app, such as income sources and amounts, bills and their due dates, credit card names with the last 4 digits (optional), statement amounts, extras, cash on hand (each amount added or taken out, with its date, an optional note, and who added it), payments you mark as paid, currencies and exchange rates.
  • Group details: the group's name, its invite code, who its members are, and requests to join it (the name you give when you ask to join).

AsaKwarta does not connect to your bank or cards, and never asks for full card numbers, bank logins or PINs. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use your location, contacts, photos or camera.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your plan safe in your account.
  • To share the same plan with the people in your group, and to let the group owner approve who joins.
  • To remind you before payables are due. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no reminder content is sent to a server.

Where it is stored

Your account and plan are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, in its Southeast Asia (Singapore) region. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Database rules allow only members of your group to read or change your group's plan.

The app also keeps a copy of your plan on your phone so you can view it offline. Uninstalling the app removes that copy.

If you use Back up now, the app creates a file and hands it to the place you choose, such as Google Drive. That copy is then under your control.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. It is shared only with:

  • the people in your group, who see the same plan and each member's name and sign-in email. Nobody joins without the group owner's approval;
  • Supabase, which stores it for us;
  • Google, only if you choose Google sign-in;
  • authorities, only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed right away from our live database. Encrypted weekly backups of the database are kept for up to 90 days for disaster recovery, so a copy may remain there until that backup expires. Backups are never used for anything else.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This policy is also linked from Settings → Privacy policy. You can also ask us to do it: see Delete your AsaKwarta account.

If you are the only member of your group, the group and its whole plan are deleted. If you share the group with others, your sign-in is removed and the plan stays for them.

Your rights

You can see and change your data in the app at any time, export it with Back up now, and delete it as described above. You can also email us to ask what we hold about you. If you are in the Philippines, these rights come from the Data Privacy Act of 2012; in Singapore, from the PDPA.

Children

AsaKwarta is meant for adults (18 and over) managing household money, and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
